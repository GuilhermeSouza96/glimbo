Glimbo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Glimbo is a block puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what data is used when you play, by whom and why. In short: we do not collect any of your data. The ads in the game are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data, as explained below.

1. What stays on your device

Your progress (scores, levels, coins, power-ups, achievements, themes) and your settings are saved only on your device. The game has no sign-up or login and does not send this data to any server of ours. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google service. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and interactions with ads.

This data goes directly to Google, which handles it under its own policy: how Google uses information from partner apps.

Consent: in countries and regions where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in SETTINGS → PRIVACY OPTIONS. If you do not consent, ads still appear, but they are not personalized.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads).

3. Purchases (Google Play)

In-game purchases (coins, starter pack, remove ads) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so it can deliver what you bought. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children

Glimbo is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone, of any age.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, under the law of your country (such as the GDPR in Europe and Brazil's LGPD). We do not store personal data. For the data used for ads, use the options in section 2 or Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. For any request or question, write to the contact below.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The current version is always on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
