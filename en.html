Glimbo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Glimbo is a block puzzle game for Android. This policy explains, as completely as possible, what data is used when you play, by whom, what for, for how long, and how you can refuse or ask for deletion.

In short:

  • The game does not ask for or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress stays on your device and, only if you sign in to Play Games, also in your Google account.
  • The game uses Google services to show ads (AdMob), save your progress in the cloud (Play Games), understand how the game is used, fix crashes and open seasonal events (Firebase), and process purchases (Google Play).
  • We do not sell your data. The only advertising use is AdMob's ads, explained in section 3.1.
  • Where the law requires it, personalized ads, usage statistics and crash reports are only used with your consent, which you can change in SETTINGS → PRIVACY OPTIONS.

1. Who is responsible

Glimbo is developed by Guilherme Souza, who is responsible for the processing of the data (the controller, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law, LGPD, and the GDPR) and is also the data protection contact. Contact: glimbo.contato@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Your progress (scores, bests, levels and stars, coins, helpers, achievements, themes, prizes) and your settings (language, sound, music, vibration) are saved in the game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access. We have no access to this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless you use cloud save (section 3.2).

3. The Google services used by the game

The data below goes directly from your device to Google. We have no servers of our own. For statistics (3.3), Google acts as Glimbo's service provider, processing the data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms. The game does not use browser cookies: instead, Google's services use identifiers and data stored on the device (such as the advertising ID and the installation ID), explained below.

3.1 Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: videos you choose to watch in exchange for a reward, and ads between games and when leaving ZEN mode (which do not appear for players who bought "remove ads"). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • the device's advertising ID;
  • the IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, system, language) and interactions with ads.

Google uses this data under its own policy: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. With personalized ads, Google uses this data to choose ads based on your interests; some laws (such as those of US states) call this "sharing" data for advertising. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first; if you do not consent, ads still appear but are not personalized. You can change your choice in SETTINGS → PRIVACY OPTIONS, and reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Privacy → Ads).

3.2 Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Play Games (in SETTINGS → CLOUD, or automatically, if you already use Play Games on the device), a copy of your progress is kept in the Saved games of your Google account, so it comes back if you change devices or reinstall the game. To know whether the progress belongs to the same account, the game keeps your Play Games player ID on the device (a Google code, not your name or email). The cloud copy only contains game progress; purchases are not stored in the cloud (they stay with your Play Store account). We do not receive your name, email or account data. Cloud save is optional: if you do not sign in to Play Games, nothing is sent. You can delete Glimbo's data from your account in the Google Play Games app or settings. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Leaderboards and achievements: with Play Games connected, the game also sends to your Google account your scores (classic, daily challenge, modes and adventure stars) and the achievements you unlock. Leaderboards are public: other players see your score together with the name and avatar of your Play Games profile. You control who sees your profile and activity in the privacy settings of the Google Play Games app. We do not receive your name or email; Google is the one that shows the leaderboards.

3.3 Usage statistics and crashes (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics)

To understand how the game is used (for example, at which level people stop, which modes are played the most, whether something became too easy or too hard) and to find and fix crashes, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect:

  • game events: games and levels started, finished or abandoned, mode, score, best, stars, moves, duration, coins earned and spent (and on what), helpers used, how "no space" was solved, ads watched, plus the first open, sessions and game updates, which Firebase records by itself;
  • in-game player characteristics: the theme in use, how many levels have been beaten, whether the player signed in to Play Games and whether they bought "remove ads";
  • a random ID for the game installation, which is not your name, email, phone number or advertising ID, and which changes if you reinstall the game;
  • device information: model, manufacturer, Android version, language, screen size, game version and the country or approximate region, derived from the IP address (the IP address itself is not kept in the statistics);
  • crash reports: when the game closes because of an error, a technical report (what the game was doing, the version, the device, free memory and when the crash happened).

This data is not sold, and the game does not use it for personalized ads. We only see reports and numbers, without knowing who each player is. Under the Google Analytics data sharing settings, Google may use this data, in aggregated and anonymous form, for benchmarking across apps, and also when we ask for technical support. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3.4 Purchases (Google Play)

In-game purchases (coins, starter pack, remove ads) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; the game only receives from Google Play the confirmation that the purchase was made, to deliver what you bought. See the Google Privacy Policy.

3.5 Seasonal events (Firebase Remote Config)

The game's seasonal events (such as Christmas and New Year) open and close on the right dates through Firebase Remote Config. To do this, the game checks with Firebase when it opens, at most once an hour. The check sends the random installation ID (the same one as in section 3.3), the game's name and version, the Android version, and the language, country and time zone set on the device, and, if statistics are turned on, the in-game player characteristics from section 3.3; Google also receives the IP address, as with any connection. The answer only says which events are open and until when. This check is part of how the game works and happens even without consent to statistics, but it is not used for statistics or ads. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. What the game does not collect

The game does not ask for or collect: name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, contacts, photos, videos, files, messages, camera, microphone, precise location (GPS), the list of other installed apps or browsing history. It also has no chat and no content created by other players.

7. Sharing and sale

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not give it to anyone other than the Google services described in section 3. We could be required to disclose data by law or court order, but we ourselves do not keep data that identifies you.

8. Transfers to other countries

Google's services store and process data on servers in several countries, including the United States. Google uses mechanisms recognized by data protection laws for these transfers (such as standard contractual clauses).

10. Security

Data sent to Google's services travels encrypted (HTTPS). On the device, progress is kept in the game's private storage. No system is completely immune to failures, but since we do not keep personal data, there is no database of ours that could leak. If a security incident ever affects players' data, we will notify the authorities and players (on this page and in the release notes), as the law requires.

11. Your choices

  • Personalized ads, statistics and crash reports: where the law requires it, in SETTINGS → PRIVACY OPTIONS (the option appears when it is available to you).
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android's settings.
  • Cloud save: do not sign in to Play Games, sign out, or delete Glimbo's data in Play Games.
  • Everything: uninstalling the game deletes the data on the device and the installation ID; you can also write to the contact in section 1 to ask for the deletion of whatever is possible. Step by step: how to delete your data.

12. Children

Glimbo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to the contact in section 1. In Europe and other places where the law sets a higher age for digital consent (up to 16), anyone under that age may only accept personalized ads and statistics with the permission of a parent or guardian.

13. Your rights

Depending on the law of your country, you have the right to: confirm whether we process your data and access it; correct it; ask for it to be anonymized, blocked or deleted; ask for portability; know who it is shared with; refuse or withdraw consent at any time (without affecting what was done before); and object to processing based on legitimate interest. This includes the LGPD (Brazil), the GDPR (Europe) and the laws of US states such as California (CCPA/CPRA). Exercising any of these rights changes nothing in the game for you. To exercise these rights, write to the contact in section 1; we reply within 15 days. Since we do not keep data that identifies you, we may ask for information to find the data in Google's services (for example, your Play Games account). You can also complain to the data protection authority of your country (in Brazil, the ANPD).

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game changes. The current version is always on this page, with the date of the last update at the top. Important changes will also be announced in the release notes on the Play Store. Previous versions are kept and can be requested through the contact in section 1.
