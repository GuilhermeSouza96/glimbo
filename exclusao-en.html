Glimbo: how to delete your data

Glimbo: Block Puzzle, developed by Guilherme Souza · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Glimbo has no sign-up and does not ask for your name or email. The game's data stays on your device and in Google services, as explained in the Privacy Policy. Below is how to delete each of them.

1. Progress and settings on the device

How to delete: uninstall Glimbo (on Android: hold the game icon → Uninstall), or clear the app's data in Settings → Apps → Glimbo → Storage → Clear data.

What is deleted: all progress (scores, levels, coins, helpers, achievements, themes), the settings and the random installation ID used in the statistics. Immediately.

2. Cloud save (Google Play Games)

It only exists if you signed in to Play Games in the game.

How to delete: in the Google Play Games app, open your profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data and choose Glimbo in the list of games (to delete only its data). If you cannot find the option, write to us (section 4) and we will help.

What is deleted: the copy of your progress kept in the Saved games of your Google account, by Google itself.

3. Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

This data has no name, email or account: it is only linked to a random installation ID, which we cannot link to you.

How to delete: uninstalling the game or clearing its data (section 1) deletes this ID from the device; what was already sent is no longer linked to the device. You can also ask for deletion by email (section 4).

What is deleted and when: Google automatically deletes the statistics linked to the ID after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.

What is kept: only numbers added up across all players (for example, "how many games were played in October"), which do not identify anyone.

4. Ask for deletion by email

Write to glimbo.contato@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Glimbo data", saying what you want deleted. We reply within 15 days. Since we do not keep data that identifies you, we may ask for some information to find the data in Google's services (for example, your Play Games account).

5. What is not deleted

  • Purchases: the record of purchases stays with Google Play, which keeps it for legal and tax reasons. It is handled by Google, not by us.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): the data used for ads stays with Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) and manage your data at myadcenter.google.com.
